DOGS

About

Dogs are highly intelligent, loyal domesticated mammals and one of the most popular pets worldwide, renowned as "man's best friend" for their companionship. Descended from wolves, they possess a keen sense of smell and hearing, and they serve humans in various roles, including guarding, therapy, and acting as service animals. Known for their unconditional love and playful nature, dogs thrive on human interaction and bring joy to households while offering security.
